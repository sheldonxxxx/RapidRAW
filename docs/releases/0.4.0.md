# RapidRAW Fork 0.4.0

The application is version 0.4.0. The native MCP bridge and npm MCP host remain independently versioned; this release keeps the compatible npm host at `@sheldonxxxx/rapidraw-mcp@0.2.0`.

## Highlights

- Manage AI repairs and local adjustments in one **Local Edits** workspace. Create, reorder, hide and remove edits, refine their selections, and copy supported selections between a repair and an adjustment without coupling their later changes. Shape Light and Surface Colour selections are adjustment-only.
- Send the active crop and matching selection to compatible AI Connectors. Generated pixels are mapped back through the photo's crop and orientation, while the surrounding original scene remains intact.
- In the Inpaint studio, the last result is applied automatically after a full batch completes. Stopped or failed batches keep completed candidates in history without applying one automatically. Candidate deletion now requires a second click to confirm.
- Reduce stale masks and repeated preview work with content-sensitive cache keys, bounded mask bitmap caching, and reusable Marigold surface components.

Generative editing requires a separately configured provider and model assets. Review connector data-transfer behavior before use and inspect generated edits at native size. Source-build and CI results do not establish fresh-machine acceptance of every packaged download.
